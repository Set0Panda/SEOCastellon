Three leaks I see again and again

Many customers look at your Google profile before they call or visit. If something is wrong there, you feel it in the business and don't know why.

Wrong opening hours

Holiday hours missing or out of date. Customers turn up to a closed door, or stay away because they think you're shut.

Unanswered reviews

A friendly, careful reply tells whoever reads it a lot about your business, even when the review is a bad one.

No easy way to get in touch

If booking or messaging on WhatsApp takes more than a tap, some customers give up.
